Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026 · Wall Bounce for Android

This Privacy Policy describes how Wall Bounce, published by Auntie Em's Games & More, handles information when you use the Android game.

Information you provide

The current testing version does not require an account, name, or other personal information to play.

Game data stored on your device

Wall Bounce saves settings and progress locally on your device, including scores, unlocked stages, achievements, selected skins, and Bounce Coins. This information is used to keep your progress between play sessions.

Advertising and analytics

The current testing version does not integrate a live advertising or analytics service. In-game options that refer to advertising are not connected to real third-party advertisements. If advertising or analytics services are added in a later version, this policy will be revised before that version is released.

Data sharing

The current game is not designed to send locally saved gameplay progress to the publisher or share it with advertisers. Your device's operating system and app distribution provider may process information under their own policies.

Children

Wall Bounce does not ask players to submit personal information. If you are a parent or guardian, you can manage the game and device settings through Android's parental controls.

Data retention and deletion

Your locally stored game progress remains on the device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, subject to any device backup or restore settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. Updated information and the effective date will appear on this page.
